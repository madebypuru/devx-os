# DevX logo asset rules

**Status: vector master pending.**

- Use only the approved DevX logo exported from Figma.
- Never typeset “devx” as a logo in Georgia, Aptos, Inter, or another font.
- Never redraw the logo with generative AI.
- Preserve original proportions, kerning, colors, and clear space.
- Check every slide for logo fidelity.
- Until an original vector master is stored in `assets/logo/`, omit the logo rather than fabricate it.
