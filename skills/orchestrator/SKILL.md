# DevX presentation orchestrator

1. Read the brief and identify audience, narrative and evidence.
2. Load DevX identity tokens and approved logo assets.
3. Plan slide-by-slide narrative and archetypes.
4. Apply typography and layout rules.
5. Route slides to illustration, gradient, diagram or analytics specialists **only when needed**.
6. Assemble and check slide-level pacing, visual diversity and content density.
7. QA logo fidelity, fonts, spacing, contrast, source accuracy and editable output.
8. Mark unresolved assets or unverified facts instead of inventing them.

**Rule:** A deck is not brand-compliant merely because its colors resemble DevX.
