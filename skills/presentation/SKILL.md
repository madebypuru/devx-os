# DevX presentation composition

Reference: user-provided Chronicle Project Plan (12 slides) and Marketing Campaign Planner (14 slides).

## Layout grammar
- Use editorial hierarchy and generous negative space.
- Match content to an appropriate layout: problem/solution split, matrix, timeline, team grid, table, structured narrative, or full-bleed atmospheric visual.
- Alternate dense and spacious slides; vary dark, light and visual compositions.
- Make imagery meaningful, not decorative filler.
- Prefer one clear point per slide; avoid generic cards repeated throughout.
- For launch decks: show the website, demonstrate its information architecture, and explain why the experience improves buyer understanding.
- Follow DevX tokens and original identity assets. Chronicle is **not** a source for DevX colors, fonts or logo.

## Guardrails
- Do not assert draft or placeholder performance metrics as verified.
- Do not invent customer names, case studies, endorsements or launch dates.
- Export editable elements where possible and visually QA every slide.
